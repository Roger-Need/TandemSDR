Help & Keyboard Shortcuts

Getting Started
Enter the server IP address and port 5454, then click Connect. Once connected, all controls become active. Audio begins muted — click the speaker icon or press V to enable it.
Spectrum & Waterfall Mouse Controls
Left-click Set VFO frequency at cursor (snapped to Snap value)
Right-click Set Center frequency at cursor and sync VFO
Drag freq. axis Pan center frequency (drag on the frequency label row at bottom of spectrum)
Scroll wheel Step VFO frequency up / down
